\section*{Internal Evidence Checklist}

\paragraph{Already supported by frozen artifacts.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Current geometry and confidence checkpoint identities and hashes.
    \item Full Astex, PoseBusters v2, and CASF reference-pocket N80/S25
    diagnostic with first, Vina+DG, pure-confidence, composite, and oracle
    selectors.
    \item Official PoseBusters validity for composite-selected poses.
    \item Current-stack pocket crop and center-jitter sensitivity matrix.
    \item Heavy-atom, fragment-count, rotatable-bond, and cofactor slices.
    \item Registered confidence continuation/filter studies and Vina-guidance
    negative result.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Required before making a strong generalization claim.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Retrain or confirm on the final canonical-SMILES-disjoint,
    pocket70-disjoint split with strict external-benchmark ligand exclusion;
    archive the manifest and leakage-audit output.
    \item Run representative external baselines under matched receptor,
    pocket, candidate-count, and post-processing contracts.
    \item Measure variability over independent training or sampling seeds, not
    only finite-dataset Wilson intervals.
    \item Record end-to-end runtime, peak accelerator memory, hardware,
    preprocessing time, and failure rate under one frozen inference preset.
    \item Publish a versioned code/weight/data-access archive with a DOI and
    commands that regenerate every table and figure.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{High-value mechanistic experiments.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Fragment-state versus atom- or torsion-state baseline.
    \item Direct fragment-vector head versus Newton--Euler aggregation.
    \item Removal of atom auxiliary and distance-geometry losses.
    \item Candidate-count and ODE-step scaling at matched compute.
    \item Pure-confidence official PoseBusters validity.
    \item Target-independent pocket centers and apo or predicted receptors.
    \item Ligand- and pocket-neighbor applicability-domain analysis.
    \item Representative successful and failed complexes with crystal,
    selected, and oracle poses shown under one visualization convention.
\end{itemize}
